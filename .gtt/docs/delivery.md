# Delivery Planning

> **Canonical reference:** https://github.com/GTT-Community/gtt-method/blob/main/GTT-CANONICAL-v3.0.md

GTT governs **what** is built. Delivery Planning adds **how long it takes and with how many hands**, before the
work starts and while it runs. It is computed from files by `.gtt/scripts/gtt-delivery.sh`: the agent explains and
proposes, you decide, and no tokens are spent on measuring.

GTT plans and watches the fronts. It never launches or orchestrates agents; that belongs to your ADE.

## The skill

The `gtt-delivery` skill (estimating, buy or build, fronts, proposals when a signal opens) reaches every ADE that reads skills, from one source: `.gtt/scaffold/skills/gtt-delivery/SKILL.md`. Claude Code reads `.claude/skills/`, Codex `.agents/skills/`, Copilot `.github/skills/` and Kiro `.kiro/steering/` (included by its description). The copies are generated by `bash .gtt/scripts/gtt-skills.sh sync --apply` and `validate` fails if one drifts or is missing for a participating ADE.

## The questions

Asked at the start (`gtt init` asks D1-D3; the Initial Design Questionnaire, section 4.7, confirms them and asks
D4-D6). One per turn, "I do not know yet" is a valid answer and nothing is invented.

| Id | Question |
|---|---|
| D1 | Who is going to build this? (me with one agent / me with several / a team) |
| D2 | How many parallel fronts can you sustain? Depends on your agent plan's usage limits. |
| D3 | When do you need a first usable version? (days / 1-2 weeks / a month or more / no date) |
| D4 | What do you prioritise? (speed / balance / everything in-house) |
| D5 | Can you use third-party services for the generic parts (login, payments, email, files)? |
| D6 | Cap per Epic before GTT warns you (8 agent hours by default) |

The answer is `.gtt/delivery.json`. It is operation, not architecture, so it can change at any time:
`gtt delivery set` (or `gtt-delivery.sh select --apply`).

## Estimating

Estimates are **agent hours plus human decision points**, never weeks of a person: `Estimate: 6h + 2 decisions`.
Until three Stories have been measured the plan says **uncalibrated**.

| Entry | Fields |
|---|---|
| Epic (approved with the Epic) | `Estimate`, `Depends on`, `Lane`, `Owner` (Team profile), `Touches`, `Build` (`own` or `integrate (ADR-NNN)`), `Budget` |
| Story (the agent's own) | `Estimate`, `Owner` (`agent` or `human`; your own actions are Stories with `Owner: human`) |

`Budget` is `ok`, `over - accepted: <reason> - <who> - <YYYY-MM-DD>`, or `over - split | cut | integrate`.
Buying a service instead of building is an architecture decision: `Build: integrate (ADR-NNN)` needs that ADR.

## Before starting: the plan

`gtt plan` prints the plan per front, the longest chain, the estimated finish and, when it does not fit,
the levers (parallel fronts, buying instead of building, a vertical slice) with the hours each saves. Every lever is a
`[PROPOSAL]`. `gtt plan --lanes` prints one summary per front (Epics, the paths it may touch, the interfaces it
depends on, its `Done when`) and the `git worktree add` commands to copy. Nothing is created.

Two Epics on different fronts must not share `Touches` unless one depends on the other through an interface.

## While working: the log and the signals

`gtt-domain/delivery-log.jsonl` is append-only. Every change of a Story or Epic status is recorded with its time,
actor and front by `gtt-delivery.sh log`, which reads the backlog: the agent has no argument to give it, and the file
is protected against writes. In Claude Code a hook runs it after each backlog write; in the other ADEs it runs at session
start and in `gtt status` (coarser resolution). It does not depend on Git.

Calibration is the median of real/estimated over the Stories closed. The finish date is what remains times that ratio,
along the longest dependency chain.

| Signal | Condition |
|---|---|
| DLV-STORY-OVER | Story In Progress for more than 1.5x its estimate |
| DLV-EPIC-BUDGET | Epic over its cap with no decision in `Budget` |
| DLV-FORECAST | Estimated finish after the deadline |
| DLV-HUMAN-WAIT | An action for you waiting more than 24 h |
| DLV-LANE-CROSS | Epics of different fronts overlap in `Touches` |
| DLV-NO-VALUE | No Epic of `Kind: slice` or `skeleton` (only with a development method declared) |
| DLV-UNCALIBRATED | Fewer than 3 Stories measured |

What each means depends on your Autonomy Profile (`profiles.json`, `delivery`). `gtt report [--epic EPIC-NNN] [--json]`
shows percent per Epic, real against estimated, the ratio, the forecast and the open signals.

## At the freeze

`gtt-delivery.sh check --pre-freeze` runs inside `gtt-freeze.sh` and `gtt-validate.sh`: every Planned Epic has an
Estimate; an Epic over its cap carries its decision; with more than one front every Epic has `Lane` and `Touches` and
the fronts do not overlap; every `integrate` names an ADR that exists; every external `[FUENTE: url, consultado AAAA-MM-DD]`
is under 90 days old. Light only warns, Medium requires the decisions, Hard and Team block. A project that never declared
delivery planning is not obstructed.
